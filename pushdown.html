Laws in effect

    Grammar

    A sentence is key=value, read left to right. The keys are you, win, stop and kill.

    Book II adds read: while any row says read=v, columns are read top to bottom as well, and whatever touches the bottom of a column joins it.     Every character in the value takes on that meaning.

    A sentence begins at its key, and anything touching its end joins the value. Type locked in a sentence is only type: it can be pushed, and nothing more.
